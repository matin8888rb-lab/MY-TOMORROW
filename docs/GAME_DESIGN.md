# MY TOMORROW — VALORA: Game Design Notes

## High concept
An offline, third-person 3D life simulator in a fictional English-inspired European town around 2000. The player creates a character, finds work, manages money and daily needs, rents and furnishes a home, meets residents and gradually builds a life.

## Visual direction
- Grounded, warm early-2000s European town atmosphere.
- Readable pale blue/green UI, restrained highlights and comfortable contrast.
- Stylized but proportionate 3D assets; no copied commercial characters, brands or locations.
- Android-first landscape presentation.

## Core loop
Explore town → meet people and discover opportunities → work or complete tasks → earn Valora Crowns (VC) → pay for food, rent and furnishings → rest and continue the next day.

## First vertical slice (0.1.0)
- Launch directly into a small town.
- Move with keyboard or touch controls.
- Find the town hall.
- Interact to complete one objective and earn 12 VC.
- The scene provides clear HUD feedback.

## Expansion sequence
1. Reliable Android build and touch controls.
2. Character creator and persistent save/load.
3. Daily needs, rest and first home.
4. Job locations and work shifts.
5. NPC schedules and dialogue.
6. Larger town, shops, quest progression and economy.

## Product constraints
- Offline-first; no mandatory account or network.
- Free core experience and no hidden paid dependencies.
- Optimize for mid-range Android hardware.
- Keep saves versioned when persistence is introduced.
